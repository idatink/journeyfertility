# Egg freezing: UK vs abroad — what women actually say

_Research note. Source: first-hand threads on r/eggfreezing, r/AskUK, r/eggfreezingabroad,
read July 2026. Directional qualitative signal, not a survey._

## TL;DR
Cost is the overwhelming reason UK women look abroad. Once abroad is on the table, the
decision hinges on **trust** (reviews, safety, communication), **per-age success data**, and a
**realistic all-in total** (treatment + meds + storage + travel + shipping eggs home) — the
exact spreadsheet people build by hand. That all-in comparison is JourneyFertility's wedge.

## Why they go abroad
- **Cost, overwhelmingly.** UK: ~£5–6k a round is typical; London packages £16k–£27k; meds
  ~£2k; storage £200–£400/yr. Abroad: Spain ~€2,600/cycle, Greece ~€1,830 + €1–1.2k meds,
  Barcelona "under 4k + meds", Czech/Poland cheaper. One UK commenter did **three** Czech
  rounds + 3 yrs storage incl. travel & hotels for less than one London package. A Canadian:
  ~$20k at home vs $4–5k in Spain.
- **Being there anyway / flexible travel** (e.g. airline staff flying free; long solo trips).
- **Easier, faster access** — clinics abroad accept them within their timeframe; less gatekeeping.

## How they choose a clinic
1. **Review / horror-story vetting.** Top fear is trust. They hunt the *buried* 1-star reviews
   among the 5-stars — mixed-up blood samples, safety incidents, "loss of communication after
   payment." Safety and responsiveness outweigh price.
2. **Per-age success rates.** They seek per-age, per-transfer live-birth data (Spain's
   reproduccionasistida.org is recommended for exactly this), not headline numbers.
3. **Peer experience.** Search subreddits *by country* for first-hand cost + experience
   write-ups; WhatsApp groups; referral discounts.
4. **Realistic all-in total.** They build spreadsheets and post "plan check please": treatment +
   meds (dose depends on AMH) + cryo-per-oocyte + annual storage + **shipping eggs back to the
   UK (~£3k)** + flights + ~2 weeks' accommodation for monitoring.
5. **Logistics & clinic feel.** Monitoring stay length (~2 wks), Airbnb min-stay issues, whether
   early scans can be done at home, personal-vs-impersonal clinic style.

## UK-local backdrop
- Strong skepticism about odds: ~39% overall live birth; 51% if frozen under 38; 70% if under 38
  **and** 20+ eggs. Widely understood as "not the insurance it sounds like" (thaw + fertilise +
  FET later adds ~£6k).
- NHS funding varies by postcode/age (Scotland more generous).
- **Employer benefits** come up a lot: Apple/Meta reportedly up to £16k for London staff, via
  named providers **Carrot, Peppy, Fertifa**; some employers up to £50k. → sourced-worthy leads
  for the employer-benefits guide.
